For operators · teams · advisors

AI business in a box — with a human in the loop.

Denizen is a visual operating layer for AI agents: a staffed digital office where agents do real work (research, intake, security, code, CRM-facing tasks) and you supervise like a manager — not another chatbot, not a black-box automation.

Who it’s for

Teams running multiple AI agents who need visibility and control — ops, founders, and orgs that can’t manage work from logs alone.

Human in the loop

Agents act on the floor. You walk in, talk to them, approve escalations, and jump in when judgment is required. Autonomy with oversight.
