Meet Buzz.

A small phone that floats over the floor. Click it, a ring opens, and whatever it catches goes to Abby with one line from you. You see it before it goes.

  • Screenshot. Drag a box, add a line, Send. On Desktop the picture stays on your disk and Abby gets the path. In the browser you see it first, then the office stores a copy so she can see it.
  • Record message. Speak, read it over, then it goes.
  • Record meeting. The room in parts, transcribed as they land on the office speech endpoint.
  • Dictation. Every capture opens a note card with a mic in it.
  • Leave invisible. Desktop Buzz can stay out of every screen share and recording.

One capture path for every AI on the floor. You keep the subscriptions you already have; Buzz just puts the work in one place so you spend less time (and less overlap) herding them.

Questions

Ask before you walk in.

Lucy at the desk will talk you through it. The short version is here.

What problem does this solve?

Your agents do real work, but you only see it as text. Denizen puts that work on a floor you can watch: who’s idle, who’s shipping, who needs you.

How is this different from logs or a dashboard?

Logs say something happened. Dashboards pile up metrics. Denizen is spatial — you read the room like a manager walking the floor, then open the trail only when you need it.

Do I need new AI subscriptions?

No. Connect the models you already pay for. Less tab-hopping and less overlap, because Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and the rest share one office.

What does it cost?

Demo is free. Team starts with a conversation on Discord — no card on this page. Desktop is the station install.

Do I need Desktop?

Browser office is enough to walk the floor and Buzz. Desktop is for the overlay plus local files and models.

Does the laptop have to stay on?

Yes for Desktop station work. Lid-closed jobs are listed under Coming.

Do I pay for new models?

No. Seat the subscriptions you already have. Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Grok, and LM Studio share one floor.

Is Buzz free?

Yes. Buzz is part of the office — screenshot, record, meeting, dictation. Desktop adds always-on-top and leave-invisible. Nothing about Buzz is locked behind a paid plan.

Does my code leave the laptop?

Souls and memory live on disk. Desktop screen and files are off / ask / trusted. A hosted office still needs a copy of a browser screenshot so Abby can see it — that is written down in TELEMETRY.md.

How do I start?

Open the live demo — no account. Watch the floor move, Buzz a note to Abby, then talk to Zion when you want a team office that persists.

Who is this for?

Solo builders juggling agents, teams that hand off work, and ops folks who want patrols and escalations visible — not buried in another tile.
