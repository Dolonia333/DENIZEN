Build your station

Bring Denizen home.

Turn this computer into a Denizen station. Your agents can help with approved local tasks, show their work in the city, and optionally add City Power while your computer is idle.

Download for Windows Download for Linux

Getting started:

  1. Download and run Denizen-Setup.exe on Windows, or Denizen.AppImage on Linux.
  2. Launch Denizen and enter the city.
  3. Choose how this station may help. Denizen remains approval-first.
  4. Add City Power now, or leave it off and decide later.
  5. Connect your Citizen ID by choosing Link this computer in the browser.
  6. Enter Denizen. Your station and web account stay connected.

Windows 10 or newer, or a modern 64-bit Linux distribution. Future updates download inside Denizen and install when the app restarts. City Power is optional and can be paused at any time.
