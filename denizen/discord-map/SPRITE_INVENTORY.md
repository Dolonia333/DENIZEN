## How game.js maps art

1. `ASSETS` object maps **logical keys** → relative PNG paths.
2. `preload` loads every `ASSETS` entry as a Phaser texture key.
3. Room kits (`KITS`, `THEME_KITS`, `decorateRoom`) pick keys by channel theme.
4. `furn()` / `placeFood()` place sprites with foot/bottom origin on floor lines.
5. NPC sheets: `NPC_SHEETS` + `NPC_POSE_DEFS` (walk idle sit lie eat wave …).
6. Pets: `PET_SHEETS` 8-dir walk on row 0.
7. Food: `FOOD_KEYS` + `seedFoodOnAllTables()` + `actorEatFood()`.
